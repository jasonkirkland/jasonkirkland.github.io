Spiral Belt Designer

Turns a straight belt profile (STL or SVG) into a standing spiral that fits your print bed.
1 · Input
Drop STL or SVG file(s) here, or click to choose.
Multiple STLs = multiple colour parts (keep them in the same coordinates).
Width = the belt's height when standing (Z in your Tinkercad screenshot).
2 · Belt size
Repeat keeps vertical pin-stripes the same size & spacing (load one repeat of the pattern). Stretch scales the whole profile to the length.
3 · Pin stripes (optional)
Generated by the app as separate colour parts. They follow your profile's real surface, so they hug lips and fillets.
Raised = sticks out past the surface. Inlay = sinks into the belt (overlaps it, so the colour goes into the wall). Set both to 0 to turn a stripe off.
4 · Spiral & printer
Gap ≥ 1 mm keeps the turns from fusing. Smaller ° = smoother curve, bigger file.
5 · Export
Multi-colour: download each part, then drag them all into Bambu Studio / Snapmaker Orca at once and answer Yes to "load as a single object with multiple parts".
Load an STL / SVG profile — or click “Load demo belt” — to see the spiral.
