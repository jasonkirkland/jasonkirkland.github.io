How to play

  • Tap a card to send it to the best open spot.
  • Drag it instead to choose the spot yourself.
  • Tap the empty felt to hide the toolbar, and again to bring it back.
  • Any card can land on a card one rank higher, of any suit.
  • Only cards already in a same‑suit run move together.
  • Tap the stock pile (bottom right) to deal a row — every column must have at least one card first.
  • Score starts at 500: −1 per move, +100 per completed suit.
  • Keys: N new · U undo · H hint · Space deal.
